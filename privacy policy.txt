## 3. LOCATION DATA

### 3.1 Types of Location Data

**Precise Location (GPS):**
- Collected only when you explicitly grant location permissions
- Used for accurate vehicle location display and proximity-based searches
- Can be disabled in your device settings at any time

**Approximate Location:**
- Derived from IP address or general area settings
- Used for regional content and search results
- Cannot be completely disabled while using location-based features

### 3.2 How We Use Location Data

**Core Functionality:**
- **Vehicle Mapping:** Display vehicles on interactive maps
- **Proximity Search:** Show listings near your location or specified areas
- **Location-based Filtering:** Filter search results by geographic area
- **Address Autocomplete:** Suggest addresses when creating listings

**Enhanced Features:**
- **Saved Searches:** Remember your preferred search locations
- **Recommendations:** Suggest relevant listings based on your location patterns
- **Regional Content:** Provide location-appropriate content and information

### 3.3 Location Data Controls

**User Controls:**
- **Permission Management:** Grant or revoke location permissions through device settings
- **Selective Sharing:** Choose when to share your location for specific features
- **Location Accuracy:** Choose between precise and approximate location sharing
- **Data Deletion:** Request deletion of stored location data

**Background Location:**
- We do not collect location data when the app is not actively in use
- Location tracking stops when you close the app or disable location services
- No continuous or background location monitoring

### 3.4 Google Maps Integration

We use Google Maps Platform services, which means:
- Location queries are processed by Google's servers
- Google's privacy policy applies to Google Maps usage
- Map data and search queries may be subject to Google's data collection practices
- You can review Google's privacy policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 7. DATA RETENTION

### 7.1 Retention Periods

We retain your personal information for different periods based on the type of data and purpose:

**Account Information:**
- **Active Accounts:** Retained while your account is active
- **Inactive Accounts:** Deleted after 3 years of inactivity (with prior notice)
- **Closed Accounts:** Deleted within 30 days of account closure (except as required by law)

**Vehicle Listings:**
- **Active Listings:** Retained while listing is active
- **Expired/Sold Listings:** Archived for 1 year, then deleted
- **Deleted Listings:** Removed immediately upon user request

**Communication Data:**
- **Messages:** Retained for 2 years to facilitate dispute resolution
- **Support Communications:** Retained for 3 years for quality assurance
- **Marketing Communications:** Retained until you unsubscribe

**Technical Data:**
- **Usage Analytics:** Aggregated data retained indefinitely (anonymized)
- **Log Files:** Retained for 1 year for security and diagnostic purposes
- **Error Reports:** Retained for 2 years for app improvement

### 7.2 Legal Retention Requirements

Some information may be retained longer when required by law:
- Tax and financial records: As required by Tunisian law
- Legal proceedings: Until resolution of any legal matters
- Regulatory compliance: As mandated by applicable regulations
